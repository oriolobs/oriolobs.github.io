05 — Rules of engagement

Nothing starts without written authorization

Each engagement is governed by a signed authorization that defines the targets, the permitted techniques and the testing dates. Only customer-owned systems, or systems the customer is explicitly authorized to have tested, are in scope.

  1. 01

    Scope & authorization

    Targets, permitted techniques, testing window and emergency contacts agreed and signed.

  2. 02

    Discovery & exploitation

    Vulnerability discovery and controlled exploitation, limited to authorized targets and dates.

  3. 03

    Validation & reporting

    Proof-of-concept, impact validation and attack-path analysis, with clear remediation guidance.

  4. 04

    Retest

    Remediation verification once fixes are in place, with an updated status for every finding.
